# Recipe Catalogue

A personal catalogue of recipes collected from YouTube and other websites, kept under the owner's control and adapted to their taste.

## Language

**Catalogue**:
The owner's whole collection of Recipes.
_Avoid_: Library, cookbook, collection

**Recipe**:
One dish in the Catalogue, written out in full by the owner, with every quantity in a Standard Unit.
_Avoid_: Entry, item, post

**Source**:
A link to where a Recipe was originally found. It is a credit only; the Recipe never depends on the Source still existing.
_Avoid_: Original, reference, origin

**Notes**:
Free text on a Recipe where the owner records tweaks and observations.
_Avoid_: Comments, variations, my version

**Tag**:
A free-form label the owner attaches to Recipes to group them, such as "dinner" or "air fryer".
_Avoid_: Category, label

**Photo**:
An image of a Recipe supplied by the owner, never copied from the Source.
_Avoid_: Picture, thumbnail

**Ingredient**:
One line of a Recipe's ingredient list: a name with a quantity in a Standard Unit, a plain count, or "to taste".
_Avoid_: Item, component

**Ingredient Group**:
A named subset of a Recipe's Ingredients, such as "For the sauce".
_Avoid_: Section, sub-recipe

**Step**:
One numbered instruction in a Recipe's method.
_Avoid_: Instruction, direction

**Standard Unit**:
A unit a quantity may be written in: grams, millilitres, litres, or the cup (250 ml). Spoon measures, pinches and US cups are not Standard Units.
_Avoid_: Measure, metric unit

**Normalising**:
Rewriting a pasted recipe so that every quantity is in a Standard Unit, a plain count, or "to taste".
_Avoid_: Converting, cleaning up

**Rating**:
The owner's single, editable 1–5 star opinion of a Recipe.
_Avoid_: Score, stars, review

**Cook**:
One occasion the owner made a Recipe: a date, optionally with a note about that occasion.
_Avoid_: Attempt, session, entry

**Log**:
The list of a Recipe's Cooks. How many times a Recipe was made is the number of Cooks in its Log.
_Avoid_: History, counter, journal

**Backlog**:
Ideas for future features, written up so they're ready to explore, but not part of the first version.
_Avoid_: Roadmap, wishlist
